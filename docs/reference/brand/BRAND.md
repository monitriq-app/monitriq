# Monatriq Brand Pack

## Brand
**Monatriq**

Primary brand line: **Know today. Go further.**

Supporting product line: **Smarter money. A brighter tomorrow.**

## Logo system
- `monatriq-logo-primary.svg` — primary horizontal logo for light/neutral backgrounds
- `monatriq-logo-dark.svg` — gradient mark + dark wordmark
- `monatriq-logo-white.svg` — gradient mark + white wordmark for dark backgrounds
- `monatriq-logo-mono-dark.svg` — single-colour dark logo
- `monatriq-logo-mono-white.svg` — single-colour white logo
- `monatriq-logo-stacked.svg` — stacked logo
- `monatriq-mark-gradient.svg` — primary standalone mark
- `monatriq-mark-dark.svg` / `monatriq-mark-white.svg` — single-colour marks
- `monatriq-app-icon.svg` — app icon treatment
- `favicon.svg` — small browser mark

## Core colours
- Midnight Navy — `#0B1F3B`
- Obsidian — `#071820`
- Deep Teal — `#0E6F62`
- Electric Teal — `#00D1B2`
- Cool Aqua — `#7EE3E0`
- Deep Blue — `#0A5EA8`
- Warm Off-White — `#F8F7F2`
- Mist Grey — `#D8DEE4`
- Ink — `#0B172A`

## Usage
Keep clear space around the mark of at least 20% of the mark's width.
Do not rotate, skew, recolour individual facets, add outlines, or separate the fold pieces.
Use the white wordmark on dark surfaces and the dark wordmark on light surfaces.
For very small sizes, use the standalone mark rather than the full wordmark.

## Production note
The wordmark in the supplied SVG files is converted to vector outlines, so the logo does not depend on a locally installed font.
