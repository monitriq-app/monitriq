# Monitriq Brand

## Brand
**Monitriq**

Monitriq is the current product name. Historical implementation records may refer to the former name Monatriq.

Primary brand line: **Know today. Go further.**

Supporting product line: **Smarter money. A brighter tomorrow.**

## Canonical logo artwork
The approved Monitriq 2D brand asset pack is the canonical and final source of Monitriq logo artwork. It is stored, exactly as supplied and unmodified, at:

`docs/reference/monitriq brand/`

The M + rising-arrow artwork must never be redrawn, regenerated, recreated with CSS, retyped as a font wordmark, or otherwise reinterpreted. Use the supplied files directly. The pack's SVG files are image-preserving wrappers around the approved artwork.

## Production assets (`public/brand/`)
Byte-identical copies of the supplied files:

- `monitriq-logo-primary-transparent.svg` — primary stacked logo, dark wordmark (light/neutral backgrounds)
- `monitriq-logo-white-transparent.svg` — stacked logo, white wordmark (dark backgrounds)
- `monitriq-logo-horizontal-transparent.svg` — horizontal logo, dark wordmark (used by the app on the light theme)
- `monitriq-logo-horizontal-white-transparent.svg` — horizontal logo, white wordmark (used by the app on the dark theme)
- `monitriq-mark-transparent.svg` — standalone mark (used in the app header)
- `monitriq-app-icon-192.png`, `monitriq-app-icon-512.png` — PWA/app icons (also the Apple touch icon; the pack ships no separate apple-touch-icon file)
- `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-64.png` — browser favicons (the pack ships no favicon.svg)

The pack also contains PNG equivalents of the logos, `monitriq-app-icon-1024.png`, and a `README.txt`; these remain in the reference directory only.

## Archived former-name artwork
The `png/`, `svg/` folders and `manifest.json` in this directory are the archived former-name (Monatriq) artwork pack. They are retained only as a historical reference and are not referenced by production code. `brand-tokens.css` in this directory remains the canonical source of the colour tokens (the CSS custom-property prefix is `--monitriq-*`; the colour values are unchanged).

## Usage
Keep clear space around the mark of at least 20% of the mark's width.
Do not rotate, skew, recolour individual facets, add outlines, or separate the mark's pieces.
Use the white-wordmark variants on dark surfaces and the dark-wordmark variants on light surfaces.
For very small sizes, use the standalone mark rather than the full wordmark.
