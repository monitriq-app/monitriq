## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-conviction personal wealth management, engineered for private wealth clients, family office principals, and sophisticated investors seeking total financial situational awareness on mobile devices. 

The emotional resonance is calibrated for calm authority, calculated precision, and discreet luxury. The visual architecture rejects noisy fintech gamification and neon speculative charts in favor of a sovereign "financial cockpit" aesthetic: deep void backgrounds, surgical layout discipline, subtle ambient edge-glows, and restrained luxury accents.

### Design Movement: Modern Technical Luxury & Glass Depth
The style merges **Minimalist Precision** with **Atmospheric Layering**:
- Base canvases are anchored in deep, layered charcoals and slates rather than pure pitch black, preventing harsh optical clipping while preserving profound depth.
- Tactile accents draw from muted noble metals (champagne gold, amber), surgical biocentric greens (sovereign emerald), and navigational cyans.
- Structural elements use ultra-fine 1px ghost borders and faint inner highlights to suggest machined sapphire glass and structural titanium panels.
- Micro-interactions are brief, dampened, and weighted, prioritizing immediate legibility, generous touch targets, and tabular data integrity.

## Colors

The palette operates on a calibrated luminance model tailored for low-light legibility and sustained visual comfort. The color system deliberately separates functional indicators from atmospheric surfaces.

### Semantic Accents
- **Primary Sovereign Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Denotes compounding equity, cash inflows, positive yield delta, and verified secure statuses.
- **Secondary Champagne Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Denotes tactical capital allocations, liquidity movements, alternative assets, and threshold alerts requiring strategic decisions.
- **Tertiary Horizon Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**: Used for long-term forward projections, portfolio rebalancing models, targets, and telemetry points.
- **Critical Risk Coral (`#F43F5E`)**: Reserved strictly for critical leverage warnings, margin exposure, down-draw alerts, and irreversible transactional authorizations.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0F17`)**: Deep obsidian base canvas creating infinite optical ground.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#131A26`)**: Primary card panels, cockpit modules, and navigation sheets.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1C2433`)**: Interactive pills, nested card cells, secondary trays, and form controls.
- **Surface Stroke (`#232D3F`)**: Structural 1px hairline boundary separating surfaces with zero visual mud.
- **Surface Stroke Active (`#334155`)**: Focused state strokes and high-priority borders.

### Text Contrast Hierarchy
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`)**: High-contrast pure white-slate for all numerical values, balances, and titles.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`)**: Mid-tier slate for metric descriptions, column headers, and active metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`)**: Low-contrast metadata, disabled states, and auxiliary timestamps.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headlines with **Inter** for data layers and running text.

### Tabular Numerics & Financial Legibility
- Every currency display, asset count, portfolio percentage, and timestamp must activate tabular numbers via `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1, "cv11" 1` to guarantee vertical decimal alignment across multi-row asset statements.
- `label-sm` utilizes subtle uppercase transformation and widened letter-spacing (`+0.04em`) when identifying macro market tags, ticker symbols, and micro status pills.
- Headline weights are kept strictly between `600` and `700` to maintain muscular structure without ballooning into casual or playful shapes.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is constructed primarily as an edge-to-edge mobile PWA experience with a mobile-first column matrix:
- **Mobile PWA Canvas (< 640px)**: 4-column layout with fixed 16px (`1rem`) outer screen margins and 16px column gutters. Minimum touch boundary for any interactive target is strictly 48×48px.
- **Tablet / Expanded Cockpit (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins and 20px gutters. Secondary asset feeds and trend panels stack into dual-column cards.
- **Desktop / Family Office Dashboard (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of 1280px centered on screen, 32px gutters and variable auto-margins.

### Touch Ergonomics & Safe Zones
- Native mobile bottom safe areas (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) are reserved strictly for the floating primary command bar or critical action triggers.
- Navigation elements avoid top corners where one-handed thumb interaction introduces friction.
- Vertical cadence enforces consistent `space-md` (16px) separation between sibling modules and `space-xl` (32px) between major asset class groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-cast, opaque drop shadows that muddy dark themes. Instead, depth is conveyed through **surface tiering, hairline border luminosity, and restrained ambient glows**.

### Surface Tiering & Boundaries
- **Ground Floor (Z0)**: `#0B0F17` background.
- **Container Panel (Z1)**: `#131A26` with a crisp, low-contrast border (`1px solid #232D3F`).
- **Elevated Interactive Target (Z2)**: `#1C2433` with border `#334155` and top-edge inset highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), producing a beveled titanium finish.
- **Modal Sheets & Overlays (Z3)**: Backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and framed with `#232D3F`.

### Ambient Glows
- Positive balance surges and high-performance metrics project an ultra-diffused atmospheric back-glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`.
- Rebalance calls and capital events use amber back-glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- Outlines are never solid bright colors unless in an active, focused text input state.

## Shapes

The geometry balances executive precision with tactile mobile comfort using Level 2 roundedness:
- **Base Cards & Modules**: `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) creates a compact, framed enclosure that guides the eye without softening into toy-like silhouettes.
- **Interactive Inputs & Nested Items**: `rounded` (0.5rem / 8px) gives structural anchors to data tables, form fields, and inner row selections.
- **Badges, Status Indicators, and Action Pills**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish visual contrast against rectangular card frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Bars
- **Primary Cockpit Action**: Solid sovereign emerald (`#10B981`) background, `#0B0F17` ultra-bold text, `height: 48px`, `border-radius: 9999px`. Subtle bottom glow.
- **Tactical Secondary Action**: Dark slate fill (`#1C2433`), `1px solid #232D3F` border, `#F8FAFC` text, interactive state lightens surface to `#232D3F`.
- **Ghost / Utility Action**: Transparent background, text `#94A3B8`, with hover/active state transitioning to subtle `#131A26`.

### Cards & Cockpit Tiles
- Multi-metric tiles feature an upper label row (text muted, label-sm), a central value row using tabular numbers (`metric-headline`), and a bottom performance badge row.
- Background uses `#131A26` with hairline `#232D3F` perimeter stroke.
- Interactive cards add an inner top-edge white hairline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) to emphasize screen depth upon press.

### Pill Badges & Status Indicators
- **Positive Delta**: Muted translucent emerald fill (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), solid emerald text (`#10B981`), `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`, pill-shaped.
- **Tactical / Rebalance Required**: Translucent amber fill (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`), solid amber text (`#F59E0B`), `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.
- **Target Tracking**: Translucent cyan fill (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`), solid cyan text (`#06B6D4`), `1px solid rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Segmented data lists place horizontal separators using `1px solid #1C2433` with no outer inset.
- Row heights are calibrated to 56px minimum to ensure instantaneous thumb targeting.
- Left-aligned icon/ticker mark paired with title and category subline; right-aligned tabular currency value stacked above localized percentage or performance change.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Background `#131A26`, border `1px solid #232D3F`, font size minimum 16px to prevent unwanted iOS Safari zooming, height 48px. 
- **Focus State**: Stroke transitions to tertiary horizon cyan (`#06B6D4`) with `0 0 0 1px #06B6D4` and a subdued cyan field glow (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15)`).

### Capital Allocation Bars
- Horizontal segmented progress indicators showing asset breakdowns (e.g., Real Estate, Private Equity, Liquid Cash) using 6px tall tracks with 2px dividers and corresponding colored indicators matching the semantic tokens.
