3. WARNING POLICY (lib/domain/rules/cash-status.ts — one place, deterministic, per currency)
cashStatus() reads one currency's canonical row and returns one of: Comfortable (teal), Getting close (amber), At your limit (amber), Below your limit (red), Needs setup (amber). It classifies figures the engine already computed; it adds no protection arithmetic and changes no figure. Available-above-that shown in the status colour, always with a text label, an icon and a sentence.
- Below your limit: canonical retained deficit greater than zero (this includes negative cash). Sentence: "Your cash is X below the amount you chose to keep protected."
- At your limit: available above that is exactly zero with no deficit.
- Getting close: available above that is greater than zero but at or below the warning zone.
- Comfortable: available above that is above the warning zone.
- Needs setup: the engine reports not configured (no amount to keep). Never shows Comfortable, no amount shown; action "Set amount to keep".

4. 25% PRESENTATION THRESHOLD
WARNING_ZONE_FRACTION = "0.25" is the single constant. The warning zone begins when the money left above the protected amount is 25% or less of the protected amount (protecting 500,000: cash above 625,000 is Comfortable; above 500,000 and up to 625,000 Getting close; exactly 500,000 At your limit; below Below your limit). Exactly 25% is inside the zone; 25% plus one cent is Comfortable (tested). It is a Monitriq UI policy, not Safe-to-Deploy mathematics, is not referenced anywhere else in the codebase (tested), and cashStatus() accepts an override so it can become user-configurable later. Documented in PRODUCT_DEFINITION.md.

5. ZERO BEHAVIOR
If the amount being protected is explicitly zero there is no percentage zone: positive cash is Comfortable with neutral colour and the sentence "You haven't asked Monitriq to protect any money right now."; zero cash is At your limit (amber) with "You have no cash tracked in this currency right now."; negative cash is Below your limit (red, factual). Available above that = 0 with a positive protected amount is At your limit (amber), not red.

6. UNCONFIGURED BEHAVIOR
When no amount to keep exists for a currency: chip "Needs setup" (amber), no amount, "Choose the minimum amount of cash you want to keep so Monitriq can warn you before you reach it.", action "Set amount to keep" (48px link to Rules). Verified on Home, Rules & Commitments, Decisions, Financial Overview and the Spending Check; the Money page deliberately stays quiet for this state.

7. SEMANTIC COLOR RULES
Teal: Comfortable, within plan, positive progress. Amber: Getting close, At your limit, Needs setup, incomplete checks, a category over its plan, tied-up-in-assets badge. Red/coral: only Below your limit, cash shortfall, protected-money conflict, overdue commitments, explicit overall Budget breach. Neutral: cash totals, net worth, asset values, informational figures. Colour never depends on wealth (tested: the same relative situation gives the same status at NGN 600 and NGN 6 billion; NGN 49 million can be below the limit while NGN 100 can be comfortable). Every state carries a text label and sentence; icons and colour reinforce.
