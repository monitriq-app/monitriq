P0-E5-S3 — SPENDING DECISION ASSISTANT: "CAN I AFFORD THIS?"
================================================================

1. PHASE
P0-E5-S3 — everyday immediate-purchase check.

2. STATUS
COMPLETE, pending one manual browser review. Nothing committed or pushed. No schema, migration, RLS or RPC change.

3. OBJECTIVE
A person with no financial knowledge enters what they want to buy, the amount, currency and account, and sees what spending it today would change plus one plain, explainable suggested next step.

4. GIT PREFLIGHT
Clean working tree at the start (HEAD b11a009, the Core UI checkpoint). No unrelated changes.

5. EXISTING ENGINE AUDIT
- evaluate_proposed_cash_use(bucket, amount) (Rules): a pure, non-persisted read built on the canonical safe_to_deploy_by_currency() formula with a hypothetical delta. Returns balance before/after, Safe to Deploy before/after, the recomputed protected-goal cash, uncovered protected obligations and protected commitments after, and neutral statuses (minimum cash floor, protected goal, protected obligation) plus retained deficit. It validates that the account belongs to the caller, rejects amounts <= 0, and does not reject amounts above the balance (balance can go negative).
- safe_to_deploy_by_currency(): Safe to Deploy = max(liquid cash - max(minimum cash, protected goal cash + uncovered protected obligations), 0); status not_configured when no Minimum Cash to Keep exists for the currency.
- budget_facts_for_date(currency, date, category) (Budget, P0-E5-S1): planned/spent/remaining for total and category; empty when no live budget covers the date; planned/remaining null when nothing is planned.
- Decisions engine (Large Personal Purchase): evaluates a persisted Decision + Scenario; using it would create a record per check, which the brief prohibits.

6. REUSE VS NEW APPLICATION-SERVICE DECISION
Reused evaluate_proposed_cash_use as the cash/protection engine (no Decision/Scenario is created). Added the smallest read-only composition service, lib/domain/spending-check/, which calls three existing canonical reads and applies one suggestion policy. It reimplements no cash, Budget, Safe to Deploy, goal or commitment logic. Documented in SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md.

7. USER FLOW
Home card or Quick Add > PLAN > Check a Purchase, or /spending-check. One short form -> result: your purchase, SUGGESTED NEXT STEP with Why, WHAT WOULD CHANGE (Cash, Budget, Protected money, Upcoming commitments), "How this was calculated", then Change amount / Record this purchase / Open Decisions.

8. INPUTS
What are you thinking about buying? (optional description), Amount (required, exact-decimal string validated against the currency's decimal places, greater than zero), Currency (all supported; profile preferred preselected; currencies you already track cash in are listed first), Which account would you pay from? (required; only your own, non-archived, same-currency accounts; auto-selected when there is one; if none: "No cash account in this currency is being tracked." + Add cash account), Spending category (optional; canonical Money spending categories, excluding debt payments which have their own flow).

9. CASH IMPACT
Cash before/after are read from evaluate_proposed_cash_use (currentBalance, postUseBalance). Cannot cover = the canonical post-use balance is negative; shortfall shown. An exact-balance purchase can be covered. No overdraft or credit is assumed.

10. BUDGET IMPACT
From budget_facts_for_date for today (profile timezone, resolved by the database). Remaining after = canonical remaining minus the purchase, computed once in the domain policy with Decimal (never in JSX). Over-plan is shown as "Over budget by". Money transfers and received money do not affect it (they are excluded by the Budget read model; tested).

11. CATEGORY IMPACT
If a category is chosen and budgeted: left now, left after, or "Over category budget by". If the category has no allocation: "This category isn't in your budget." (never auto-added, no judgement). If none chosen: "Category budget impact was not checked."

12. SAFE TO DEPLOY INTEGRATION
Safe to Deploy before/after and the minimum cash are read verbatim from the Rules engine. If no Minimum Cash to Keep exists the protection check is shown as "Needs setup / Not fully configured" with a Set minimum cash action; zero is never assumed. Goal/commitment statuses are still reported without it because the engine computes them independently.

13. PROTECTED GOALS
When the purchase would eat into cash backing a protected goal, the engine's protected-goal status is "conflict" and the amount used is the increase in that account's allocation shortfall (post minus current, both canonical). Shown as "This would use X currently protected for your goals." Nothing is moved or released.

14. COMMITMENTS
Protected obligations are read from the engine's uncovered-obligation figures. If the purchase leaves a protected commitment less backed, the shortfall (canonical after minus before) is shown: "This would leave X less than the amount currently protected for known commitments." Where money is simply required to stay protected for the minimum/goals/commitments and would be crossed without a specific driver, a factual line states the protected amount, cash left and the gap. Expected income is never counted as cash.

15. MULTI-CURRENCY
A check runs in the selected account's currency only: that currency's account, Budget, minimum cash, goals and commitments. Tested: an NGN check ignores a USD Budget and USD/GBP/EUR protections; a USD result contains no NGN; there is no FX or conversion anywhere in the feature.

16. SUGGESTION POLICY
One deterministic function (decideSuggestion) over the three composed checks; five states: Proceed within your plan, Reduce the amount, Wait, Review first, More setup needed. No score, rank, risk rating or AI. Same input gives the same output (tested).

17. SUGGESTION PRECEDENCE
1 insufficient tracked cash -> Reduce the amount. 2 protected cash / commitments crossed (minimum cash, protected goal money, protected commitments, or retained-cash requirement) -> Wait, naming exactly what is crossed. 3 Budget conflict -> Reduce the amount if the overall Budget goes negative; Review first if only the selected category exceeds its plan. 4 protection check incomplete (no minimum cash) and/or 5 no Budget (or a Budget with nothing planned) -> Review first. 6 all relevant configured checks clear -> Proceed within your plan. "More setup needed" is the state for having nothing to check against (no cash account in the currency); the form shows the honest message instead of running a check. Rule for tier 3 chosen by me: overall Budget over = Reduce (reducing the amount can fix it); category-only over = Review first (other categories may have room).

18. PARTIAL-INFORMATION BEHAVIOR
Each check is labelled Checked / Needs setup / Not available. A missing Budget or minimum cash never blocks the other results and is never assumed to pass; setup gaps are always disclosed (as the reasons when they decide Review first, otherwise as caveats under a stronger reason). Proceed within your plan appears only when every relevant configured check is clear. With no Budget and no minimum cash the result is Review first with both gaps stated.

19. CALCULATION TRANSPARENCY
"How this was calculated" (MoreDetails, aria-expanded) lists TRACKED / YOUR PURCHASE / CALCULATED lines (cash before, purchase, cash after, Budget left before/after, minimum cash, tracked cash after, Safe to Deploy after), following the S3B/S3C convention. Missing-information messages are not duplicated there.

20. NO-SIDE-EFFECT GUARANTEE
The service performs reads only. Tested by snapshotting financial events, cash balances, the Budget summary, goals, obligations, rules, Safe to Deploy, decisions and goal allocations before and after multiple checks: identical. No Decision is created. The feature source contains no recordMoneySpent call.

21. MONEY HANDOFF
"Record this purchase" opens the existing Money Spent form pre-filled (amount, account, category if it is a generic category, description) through a small openSpent prefill added to Quick Add's context and MoneySpentForm (optional prop; existing behavior unchanged when absent). Nothing is recorded until the user presses Save there. Verified in the browser: no financial event was created by opening it.

36. OPEN ISSUES

- The "Reduce the amount" state does not suggest a specific amount; it states the gap (e.g. over by X).

P0-E5-S3A — ACTIVE BUDGET CORRECTNESS HARDENING
================================================================

STATUS
COMPLETE. One correctness fix; the Spending Check design and suggestion hierarchy are unchanged. Nothing committed or pushed.

ROOT CAUSE
budget_facts_for_date() (the Budget boundary built in P0-E5-S1 for future Decisions) returns the non-archived Budget covering the date, preferring an active one, and does not expose the Budget's status. The Spending Check service treated any row it returned as the current plan, so a CLOSED Budget whose month includes today could produce Budget results (including a Budget conflict) even though a closed Budget is not the user's current plan. Archived Budgets were already excluded by the function itself.

CANONICAL FIX
Only lib/domain/spending-check/service.ts (plus small type/presentation follow-ups) changed. The service now also reads the existing canonical Budget repository (listBudgets, RLS-scoped) and uses the returned facts only when the Budget they belong to is active; otherwise the facts are discarded and the closed Budget is recorded so the UI can point at it. It also resolves "today" explicitly in the profile timezone (getProfile + todayInTimezone) and passes it to budget_facts_for_date, so both reads agree on the date. No second Budget engine, no Money events re-summed or read, no schema, migration, RLS or RPC change, and budget_facts_for_date itself is untouched.

ACTIVE / CLOSED / ARCHIVED BEHAVIOR
- Active: used as before (planned, remaining, category impact, conflicts).
- Closed: ignored completely; neither passing nor failing. Budget shows "Not available - You don't have an active budget for this month, so the Budget check isn't available." The action is "Open budget" (to /budget?b=<id>) because a new Budget cannot be created for a month/currency that already has a closed one (the one-live-budget-per-month rule), and Reopen is on that page.
- Archived: ignored; behaves as no Budget with the "Create budget" action (archiving frees the month).
- No Budget: unchanged (Create budget).
Copy change: the unavailable-Budget sentence now reads "You don't have an active budget for this month" for all three cases (previously "You haven't created a budget...", which was inaccurate for a closed Budget).

SUGGESTION BEHAVIOR
Unchanged policy. A closed or archived Budget is exactly "no active Budget": no Budget conflict can arise, cash and protection are still evaluated, and when they are clear the result is Review first with the reason that the Budget was not checked (never "Proceed within your plan"). Higher-precedence results (cash, protected money) are unaffected. No silent fallback to historical Budget data.
